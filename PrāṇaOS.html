What is PrāṇaOS

PrāṇaOS is a modern breathwork and breathing wellness platform inspired by the ancient wisdom of pranayama and designed for everyday life.

From calming your mind and improving focus to preparing for sleep and building a consistent mindfulness practice, PrāṇaOS makes guided breathing simple, accessible, and intuitive.

Under development · beta demo

About the creator

I’m Krishank Joy, an aspiring web developer and student physiotherapist. I love building tools and protocols that help people take care of their health.

I’m the creator of PrāṇaOS, a personal project born from my interest in wellness, human physiology, mindfulness, and the intersection of ancient practices and modern technology.

I wanted to create a simple, beautiful space where anyone could explore breathwork without unnecessary complexity, expensive subscriptions, or distractions.

My goal is to make intentional breathing more accessible and help people build small, meaningful habits that support a more mindful life. PrāṇaOS is an ongoing journey of learning, building, and exploring how simple practices can make a difference in everyday life.
